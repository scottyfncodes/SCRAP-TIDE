# Wind chop for the ocean (GITCRAFT WorldCraft)

`public/textures/tb_sea_chop_normal.png` (256², 69 KB) is the surface detail the ocean shader (`src/world/ocean.ts`)
lays over its Gerstner swell. It is one frame of a Tessendorf FFT ocean generated by **GITCRAFT Studio,
WorldCraft `ocean`**: a 24 m tile under a 9 m/s wind from 20° (the same heading as the shader's spindrift streaks and
`WORLD.wind`), choppiness 1.3, seed 71.

The shader samples it twice: at 24 m drifting downwind, and rotated at 8.5 m drifting across it, so the two layers
never line up and the tile never reads as a repeat. The fine layer fades out by 90 m and the coarse one is damped past
~400 m, so the horizon doesn't shimmer. It replaced nine value-noise evaluations per pixel (~36 `sin()` hashes), so
the water is both more convincing and cheaper on a phone GPU.

WorldCraft's own checks on the run (`gitcraft-out/tb_sea_chop.studio.json`):
- loops in time (frame at t = period equals t = 0, error 2e-14);
- tiles in space (edge columns match within 0.0064);
- significant wave height 1.73 m, matching the Pierson–Moskowitz spectrum for the wind;
- the displaced tile GLB passes the Khronos glTF-Validator (0 errors) and renders in three.js r170.

## Files
- `gitcraft-out/tb_sea_chop_normal.png`: the shipped tile.
- `gitcraft-out/tb_sea_chop_height16_f000.png`: the 16-bit height field for the same frame.
- `gitcraft-out/tb_sea_chop_engine.png`: WorldCraft's three.js render of the displaced tile.
- `gitcraft-out/tb_sea_chop.ocean.json`, `gitcraft-out/tb_sea_chop.studio.json`: the parameters, hashes, tools and licences.

The 16-frame normal and foam flipbooks (1.5 MB), the preview GIF and the 3.7 MB displaced tile GLB are not committed
because they are regenerated exactly by the command below. The flipbook would animate the chop in time, but it is
15× the download of the single tile, which phones can't afford.

## Rebuild
```
studiocraft worldcraft_ocean {"name": "tb_sea_chop", "resolution": 256, "tile_size": 24, "wind": 9, "direction": 20,
  "choppiness": 1.3, "amplitude": 1, "frames": 16, "period": 8, "seed": 71, "out_dir": "creative/ocean/gitcraft-out"}
cp creative/ocean/gitcraft-out/tb_sea_chop_normal.png public/textures/
```
